About me

Senior Graphic Designer with 10+ years of experience in brand identity, marketing campaigns, UI/UX design, digital and print design, video editing and motion graphics. I have delivered work across fintech, F&B, lifestyle, luxury interiors and e-learning, partnering with product, marketing and engineering teams from concept to production-ready artwork.

AI video generation and AI-powered design: I use ChatGPT, Claude and Google Flow to create visual concepts, custom illustrations, AI-generated graphics and AI-generated videos, then refine them in Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, After Effects and Premiere Pro. This speeds up concept-to-delivery while keeping professional craft and brand consistency.

Core skills: Brand Identity, Campaign Design, Graphic Design, UI/UX, Landing Pages, Packaging, Catalog and Print Design, Design Systems, Motion Graphics, Video Editing, Explainer and Product Launch Videos, Lottie Animation, AI Video Generation, AI Image and Illustration Generation.

Tools: Figma, Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign, After Effects, Premiere Pro, Captivate, Adobe XD, Miro, PowerPoint, ChatGPT, Claude, Google Flow.
