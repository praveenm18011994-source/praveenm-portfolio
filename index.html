Scene 02 · The story so far

For over 10 years I've worked across brand identity, marketing campaigns, UI/UX, print, video editing and motion graphics, for fintech, F&B, lifestyle, luxury interiors and e-learning. I work hand in hand with product, marketing and engineering teams. Today I start ideas with ChatGPT, Claude and Google Flow, then finish every frame by hand in Illustrator, Photoshop, After Effects and Premiere Pro.
