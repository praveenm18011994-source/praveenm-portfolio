Concept brand · Full case study

Healthy snacks that feel like a treat.

Bitekind is a concept range of roasted, never fried snacks in stand-up pouches. I built the whole brand: logo, mascot, guidelines, six pack designs, mockups, an online store, an ordering app, a launch campaign and a brand film. Every piece of it is below.

The challenge

Healthy snacks usually look like medicine.

Shelves of "better for you" snacks lean on beige packs, tiny type and long nutrition claims. They feel like a diet, so people reach for chips instead.

The idea for Bitekind: make the healthy choice the fun one. Loud colour, a cheeky mascot and short, warm copy, while still telling you plainly what is inside: roasted, not fried, no preservatives.

01 · Identity

A logo you can almost hear crunch.

Chunky, bouncy letters for the crunch. Two leaves on top for real food. A smile under the word for the happy bite. The full board, then each part on its own.

Logo versions

One primary logo, an emblem for small spaces and a monogram for app icons and stickers. Reverse and one-colour versions cover dark and print-limited uses.

Logo use

The logo only sits on white, cream or forest green. On lime, orange or yellow it goes inside a white rounded badge so it never fights the colour.

Meet BITY

A cheeky orange snack-buddy who gives the brand a face. Each pose has a job: waving hello in onboarding, thinking in empty states, cheering at checkout, munching on packs.

03 · Packaging

Six pouches. One system.

Every pouch shares the same layout: cream top with the logo, a big flavour name, BITY in the middle and a colour band at the bottom. Only the colour, the photo and the name change, so the range reads as a family on the shelf.

06 · App

Snacks at the door, one tap away.

An ordering app for repeat snackers: log in with a phone number, browse by need, add to cart, pay and track. BITY guides you through onboarding, empty states and delivery.
