Campaign case study · Bitekind launch

Snack happy.
Feel good.

The launch campaign for Bitekind's six roasted snacks. One idea, one key visual, and 13 pieces across Instagram, stories, the web and the street.

RoleConcept, art direction, design
Year2026
ChannelsSocial, stories, web, outdoor
Pieces13 + brand film

Bitekind is a concept brand made for my portfolio. The audience, rollout and measures below are my plan, not results from a live campaign.

01 · The brief

Launch a healthy snack brand people actually want.

Goal

Get noticed

Introduce Bitekind and its six snacks to young city snackers who have never heard of it.

Goal

Change a feeling

Make healthy snacking feel like a treat, not a diet or a punishment.

Goal

Drive first orders

Send people to the website and app to try their first pack.

Who it talks to

Young city snackers, 20–35

Office workers, students and gym-goers in Bengaluru, Pune, Chennai and Mumbai. Busy, on their phones, and snacking between meals.

What they think today

"Healthy snacks are boring, expensive and taste like cardboard. When I'm hungry, I just grab chips."

02 · The insight

"People don't snack to be healthy. They snack to feel good."

Most healthy brands sell the health and make you feel guilty about everything else. But a snack is a small happy moment in the day, usually the 4 pm slump at a desk.

So Bitekind shouldn't sell health. It should sell the good feeling, and let "roasted, never fried" be the reason you can enjoy it without guilt.

03 · The big idea

Snack
Happy.

Swap the guilty snack for a happy one. Every piece shows a real snack moment, with BITY bringing the fun and the pouches doing the proof.

Tone of voice

Cheeky, not sillyHonest, not preachyShort and warmIndian, everyday

Three messages

01

Feel good

The emotional promise. A happy snack, any time.

"Snack happy. Feel good."

02

Real food

The reason to believe. Roasted, never fried, no preservatives.

"Roasted, never fried."

03

Right moment

The when. The 4 pm crunch, breakfast, the gym bag.

"Your 4 pm crunch, sorted."

04 · Campaign system

One key visual, five channels.

The key visual sets the look. Each channel then takes one message and one job.

Key visual · "Snack happy. Feel good."All six pouches, BITY and real snacks bursting out on a bright, leafy background
Instagram postsOne message per post: feel good, real food, right moment.
CarouselA 3-slide swipe story from hunger to "Shop now".
StoriesQuick, playful formats: polls, new launches, app download.
Web bannerDrives clicks to the online store.
OutdoorBillboard and bus shelter near offices and colleges.

Look

Bright daylight, fresh leaves, real snacks mid-air. Always a textured background, never plain.

Layout rules

Headline top-left in chunky Baloo 2, pouches bottom-right, logo on white or in a white badge. Nothing cropped at the edges.

BITY's role

The face of the feeling: waving, thumbs up, munching. Pouches always carry the product proof.

05 · Key visual

The whole range, one happy burst.

"Snack happy. Feel good."

  • All six pouches introduce the range at once, each in its own colour.
  • BITY in the middle gives the eye a starting point and a smile.
  • Real snacks flying out show the food is real: foxnuts, chana, nuts and granola.
  • Logo and line top-left so it reads first, then the eye moves to the product.

06 · Instagram

Three posts, three messages.

Carousel · a swipe story

Problem, proof, action. Each slide ends with a reason to swipe to the next.

07 · Stories

Fast, tappable, full screen.

Stories are where people interact, so each one asks for a tap.

08 · Web & outdoor

Big, simple, readable in two seconds.

Outdoor gets one line, the logo and the pouches. Nothing else. People see it from a moving bus.

09 · Rollout plan

Six weeks, three phases.

Weeks 1–2

Tease

  • BITY appears alone: "Hungry at 4 pm?"
  • Story polls: "Which mood are you?"
  • Billboard goes up near offices.
Weeks 3–4

Launch

  • Key visual everywhere.
  • Three posts and the carousel.
  • Web banner drives to the store; brand film goes live.
Weeks 5–6

Keep going

  • Morning Magic story and bus shelter.
  • App download story.
  • Re-share real customer snack moments.

How I'd measure it

GoalWhat to trackWhere
Get noticedReach, video views, follower growthInstagram, YouTube
Change a feelingSaves, shares, poll votes, comment tonePosts, stories
Drive first ordersBanner clicks, store visits, first orders, app installsWebsite, app

10 · Brand film

The campaign in motion.

A short film for reels and the launch post: logo, BITY and the six pouches.

What I learned

A campaign holds together when every piece carries just one message. Splitting "feel good", "real food" and "right moment" across separate posts kept each one short enough to read in a scroll.

Snack happy. Feel good.
