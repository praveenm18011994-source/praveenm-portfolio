Slim Scoop · Concept Brand · 2026

Full flavour.Light scoop.

How do you sell ice cream to people who count calories, without it ever feeling like diet food? This is the story of Slim Scoop, a brand I built end to end: from strategy and logo to packaging, a brand character, retail, a social campaign and a product film.

01 · The brief

Indulgence, without the heaviness.

"Light" usually means bland: pale packs, clinical claims, a little sad. I wanted Slim Scoop to feel like the opposite: warm, joyful and full of flavour, with the lightness shown, not shouted.

01

Low-Calorie

A lighter scoop, so the treat fits into everyday life.

02

Natural Ingredients

Real cocoa, fruit, mint and vanilla. Nothing that needs explaining.

03

Delicious Flavours

03 · Logo system

Built to flex.

The vertical lockup leads on pack fronts and signage. The horizontal version handles lids, web headers and shop fascias. The mark alone works as an app icon, avatar or sticker once people know the name.

04 · Colour

Cream, forest & a spark.

Cream says indulgent, forest says natural, terracotta adds the joy. They're used in a strict 60 / 30 / 10 ratio, so every touchpoint feels like the same brand. Tap a colour to copy its code.

0%Scoop Cream#FFF6EC · backgrounds, pack bodies
0%Forest#1F4D3A · logo, headlines, lids
0%Terracotta#EA5D37 · accents, spark
One layout, every flavour

Each flavour owns one colour. It fills the wave band on packs and cone sleeves and colours the flavour name, while the brand colours stay the same.

07 · Brand character

Meet Sana.

Sana is the everyday Slim Scoop fan: active, cheerful and never guilty about a treat. She shows that light can still be indulgent. She's who I'd tell this story through.

Her lookForest crop top and leggings, a cream hoodie and cream sneakers. She wears the brand palette.
